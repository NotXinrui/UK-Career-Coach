# Cover Letter Structure

## Gate: ask first
Do not draft until the user has answered:
1. Organisation and role title.
2. What specifically interests them about this organisation or role.
3. Which experience or angle they want to lead with.

## Five-paragraph structure (about 400–500 words, one A4 page)

1. **Strategic framing.** A point of view on the role or function, not "I am applying for…". Prompt: *What do you believe about this kind of work that a hiring manager might not expect?*
2. **Point of view in practice.** The principle behind how the user works. Prompt: *Where has that principle changed an outcome?*
3. **Evidence.** Two or three concrete examples with scope and a verifiable result. Prompt: *Which achievements from your profile map most directly to the JD's top three requirements?*
4. **Why this organisation and role.** Specific, researched, not interchangeable with any other employer. Prompt: *What did you learn about them that is not on the first page of their website?*
5. **Forward-looking close (short).** Confident, concise, no grovelling. Prompt: *What do you want to be doing in this role in a year?*

## Format rules
- No generic openers and no restating the CV line by line.
- Mirror the JD's vocabulary only where the user has the experience.
- Warm but professional; active voice.
- Default coach output is an outline plus prompts. Provide full prose only if asked, and mark it as a draft for the user to rewrite.
